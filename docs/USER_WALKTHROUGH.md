# Using Whistle

A useful first session is one company review: read the sources, identify what is missing, and record the next step. You do not need to set up every service first.

## 1. Sign in

Open [Whistle](https://whistle-henna.vercel.app/login). Use an existing cloud account, or choose **Create an account**. Email confirmation is enabled. Confirmation links, password recovery and real-account access still need testing on the hosted site; see [deployment status](DEPLOYMENT.md).

Use **Forgot password?** for cloud password recovery. Enter credentials yourself. A Gemini key belongs in server settings, not in the password field.

The separate local preview runs at `http://127.0.0.1:3000`. Local and cloud accounts have separate credentials and research, even when their email addresses match. Local records do not move to the cloud automatically.

## 2. Find your way around

| Page | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Today | Check your priorities, planned meetings and pipeline summary. |
| Reviews | Open work waiting for review, including conflicts and drafts. |
| Discover | Filter saved companies or research an industry for public leads. |
| Pipeline | See a deal's evidence, owner and next action. |
| Portfolio | Follow recorded completed holdings and company updates. |
| Library | Read reference material or open team access and connection settings. |

The top search finds saved companies and destinations. Industry research in Discover searches the selected external sources.

## 3. Add or find a company

For a company you already know, choose **Add company**. Enter its name, website/domain and a founder or contact to research. Keep unknown details empty. Optional fields are available when you need them.

An existing domain opens the existing record. Creating a record does not verify a founder or fetch every fact about the company.

For new leads, open **Discover > Deal sourcing > Industry research**:

1. Describe the industry or customer problem.
2. Open **Sources** and choose available providers.
3. Confirm that the public prompt can be sent to them.
4. Select **Research this space**.
5. Read the results, source references and coverage limits.

Keep confidential deal details out of a public search. Repository stars, launch publicity and a profile name do not prove customer adoption or founder identity. Product Hunt searches a recent feed, not its entire database. A missing result is not proof that a company does not exist.

## 4. Review the company

| Tab | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Read the summary, owner, next action and recorded activity. |
| Evidence | Inspect source excerpts, dates and claims; explain any review decision. |
| Diligence | Plan questions, assign work and record a sourced outcome. |
| Model | Inspect assumptions and calculate a scenario. |
| Memo | Write the case, objections and reasons to change your view. |
| Monitor | Compare later observations with earlier assumptions and conditions. |

A Verified label applies to a specific reviewed statement and its evidence. It does not certify an entire company or make a forecast certain. Founder-reported, Missing, Conflicted and other labels explain what you actually know.

AI research is a draft. Check whether each cited excerpt supports the statement before approving it.

## 5. Record a decision

Pipeline has Companies, Board and People views. Changing a view or filter does not change a decision.

Open a company and choose **Record decision**. Select the outcome, explain why, record the counter-case or dissent, and state what would change your mind. Saving retains that reasoning with the decision version.

**Watch** means follow the company. **Pass** means do not pursue it now. **Invest** records a judgment; approval, signed commitment and completed transaction remain separate stages. An Invest decision alone does not create a completed holding.

## 6. Return to the review

Use Reviews for unanswered questions, conflicts, drafts and due work. New evidence may justify a new decision; the old decision and what was known then remain available.

Saved model and memo versions retain their original sources and assumptions. Review a changed premise rather than judging an earlier decision with facts that arrived later.

## 7. Follow a holding

Portfolio shows recorded holdings. Open Monitor to compare updates with the investment assumptions. Check reporting periods, definitions, sources and review labels before relying on a figure.

Observations must be entered or imported. Whistle does not yet collect every company's updates or send automatic notifications.

## 8. Set up services when you need them

Open **Library > Connections & imports**, then expand **All connections & setup**. A listed service is not necessarily connected. Configured means settings are present; a completed task is needed to confirm delivery.

Calendar exports download a file. The Calendly adapter needs account-specific setup; ongoing synchronization and multi-customer OAuth remain unfinished. Hosted files, indexing, workers and paid research have separate requirements.

Use **Explore the workspace** for the walkthrough, evidence labels and terms such as thesis, diligence, MOIC and IRR. The optional fictional sample is labelled and loaded only when selected. New cloud accounts do not inherit another person's local research.
