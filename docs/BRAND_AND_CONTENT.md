# Whistle brand and writing

Whistle is an investment research workspace. Its name, labels and documents should help someone understand the work without knowing how the software was built.

## Identity

The current mark uses the supplied whistle artwork. It appears in the workspace, account screens and browser icons. White artwork is used on dark surfaces; the light header uses a readable charcoal mark. Keep the mark centered and preserve its proportions.

The wordmark is **Whistle**, with **Investment research** where a subtitle helps. The earlier bracket-and-diamond symbol was replaced. Its implementation is retained only in dated development records.

Use the warm palette in [WARM_PALETTE.md](WARM_PALETTE.md). It replaces the blueprint's earlier navy and blue choices.

## Writing

Use short sentences and name the action. A heading should tell someone what they can do next. An empty state should explain what is missing and provide one useful next step.

Keep interface names consistent: Today, Reviews, Discover, Pipeline, Portfolio, Library, and the six company tabs. Prefer **Founder directory** to **founder map** when the feature is a list. Prefer **Source journal** when entries are ordered by capture date rather than collected as a live news feed.

Avoid promotional claims, emoji, em dashes and vague claims about AI. Explain whether a result comes from saved records, a public feed, a template or a configured provider. State unknowns directly. A forecast remains an assumption even if its starting data has a source.

## Claims we can support

Describe features that exist and say when setup or testing remains. A chat connector is separate from an app integration. Configured does not mean tested. A reviewed claim does not certify the whole company.

Public pages must not invent contact details, claim compliance or promise untested retention and deletion behavior. Final hosted privacy, contact and deletion arrangements still need review.

## History and calculations
